## What RepSearch is

RepSearch is a workout-tracking, progress-comparison, and training-comparison app. It keeps the training you log, shows your own progress, places it next to privacy-protected groups of other members, and lets members publish and compare programs and workouts.

- **Your numbers are descriptions.** Standings, percentiles, tiers, figures and colors, estimated one-rep maxes, targets, and trends are calculated from sets you logged. Some early readings use estimated starting levels instead of member data, and the app labels them when it does. None of them is a measurement of your health, a prediction, or an instruction to lift a particular load.
- **Recorded results are descriptions too.** A result on a program page describes what members who ran that version logged. It does not show that the program caused the result, and RepSearch does not tell you that one program is better for you than another.
- **Programs and workouts are written by members.** The structure and the reasoning attached to a published program or workout belong to the member who published it. We do not review them for safety or for whether they suit you.
