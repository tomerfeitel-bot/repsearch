# Delete your RepSearch account and data

_Last updated: October 5, 2026._

This page explains how to delete a RepSearch account (package `com.repsearch.app`) and its linked data.

## Delete in the app

1. Sign in and open **You → Settings**.
2. Under **Delete account**, start the deletion.
3. Type `DELETE`, reauthenticate, and tap **Delete permanently**.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Request deletion without the app

Email **tomer.feitel@gmail.com** from the address on your account with the subject **“Delete my account.”** Include the account email and username if known. We verify ownership and complete verified requests within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- account and authentication records,
- workouts, sets, records, templates, programs, versions you own, enrollments, and run outcomes,
- bodyweight entries and notification tokens,
- published programs and workouts, posts, comments, likes, saves, follow relationships, and comparison partnerships,
- workout-derived benchmark and program-outcome contributions.

Aggregates are refreshed without the deleted account's contribution. Moderation reports may be retained for safety, but without the deleted user's identity. Everything else linked to the account is permanently removed.
