# RepSearch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026._

RepSearch is a workout-tracking and training-comparison app for adults on iOS and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the controls available to you. The [Terms of Use](../terms/) and the [health and safety disclaimer](../disclaimer/) also apply.

## Data we collect

- **Account data:** email, username, declared date of birth, authentication records, and optional profile details such as gender, training experience, and a bio. Supabase Auth handles passwords; RepSearch never receives your plaintext password. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier and email Apple provides.
- **Training data:** workouts, sets, exercises, notes, records, templates, programs, program runs, workout history you import from another app's export file, and progress values derived from them.
- **Bodyweight:** entries you type in. Bodyweight is used to place your lifts in a bodyweight-relative strength class and to show your own trend.
- **Community data:** programs and workouts you publish, posts you make to the feed, comments on program and workout pages, likes, saves, follows and follow requests, comparison requests and what you chose to share in them, blocks, and reports.
- **Notification token:** if you turn on alerts for follows or comparison requests, a push token for your device is stored so those alerts can be delivered. It is removed when you turn the alerts off or sign out. Rest timers and training reminders are scheduled on your device and need no token.
- **Operational data:** limited server logs, server error reports, app crash reports, and non-identifying reliability metrics used to secure and operate RepSearch. A crash report carries the error, the app version, and the device model and operating system. It does not carry your workouts, bodyweight, email, or username.

We do not read Apple Health or Health Connect, and we do not collect sleep, nutrition, recovery, body-measurement, or wearable data. We do not collect contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers. RepSearch has no ads, does not sell data, and does not use data for cross-app tracking.

## How we use data

Training data powers your workout history, charts, records, templates, programs, and personal progress.

**Benchmark contribution is optional and off by default.** If you opt in, progress values derived from your workout logs may enter minimum-size benchmark groups and the recorded-progress summaries shown on program pages. Groups are formed from training-relevant details such as the exercise, starting performance, training experience, gender, and bodyweight class. No comparison is drawn from fewer than ten members, and no summary shows an individual's values or whether a particular person is included. You can still log, see your personal progress, and browse programs when opted out.

Program summaries describe what members who ran a program logged. RepSearch does not run experiments, does not conduct health-related human-subject research, and does not claim that a program causes an outcome.

## Who can see what

- **Private to you:** workout history, records, lift trends, notes, templates, and bodyweight. These never appear in rankings, feeds, or another member's view of you unless you post or share a specific item.
- **Your profile:** other members see your username, avatar, and the profile details you leave visible. A private account shows its posts only to followers you approve.
- **Posts:** a record, tier, finished program, or finished workout reaches the feed only when you post it. Nothing is posted automatically. A post shows the snapshot saved at the time you posted.
- **Published programs and workouts:** these are public pages tied to a fixed version, with your username as the author. Saving someone's published workout copies its structure, never their performance values.
- **Member comparison:** two members see each other's shared categories only after one asks and the other accepts. Each side chooses what to show and can end the comparison at any time. Following someone does not grant comparison.

## Storage, security, and service providers

Supabase provides authentication and the hosted database; the RepSearch API, hosted on Northflank, enforces authorization and visibility. Both run in the United Kingdom. Transport uses HTTPS. Push alerts are delivered through Expo's push service and the Apple and Google notification services. Server error reports and app crash reports may be processed by Sentry. Account creation may show an anti-bot check from hCaptcha, which processes your IP address and device signals to tell people from automated sign-ups. Verification and password-reset emails are sent through Supabase or an email delivery provider. These providers act on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

On-device sessions use platform secure storage. Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint app lock is evaluated by the device; RepSearch receives only success or failure. If you use Siri or Shortcuts with RepSearch, the app answers from your own data on your device; Apple handles the voice request under its own privacy policy.

## Retention

Account-linked data is retained while your account is active. Deleting the account permanently deletes workouts, bodyweight entries, programs, templates, community content, comparison partnerships, notification tokens, and derived benchmark and program-summary rows tied to you. Aggregates are refreshed without deleted or opted-out contributions. Moderation reports may be retained without your identity for safety and abuse prevention.

## Your controls

- **Export:** You → Settings → Your data prepares a portable JSON copy of your account, training, and community data.
- **Benchmark contribution:** You → Settings → Benchmark contribution lets you opt in or out at any time. Opting out removes your derived values from future aggregate refreshes without disabling personal features.
- **Private account:** You → Settings → Private account. Follow requests are under You → Settings → People.
- **Comparison requests:** You → Settings → Compare requests controls who may ask to compare with you.
- **Notifications:** You → Settings → Notifications.
- **Account deletion in the app:** the Delete account section of You → Settings permanently deletes account-linked data after confirmation and reauthentication.
- **Account deletion without the app:** email tomer.feitel@gmail.com from the account email with the subject “Delete my account.” Verified requests are completed within 30 days. See the [account deletion page](../account-deletion/).
- **Community safety:** posts, comments, and members can be reported, members can be blocked, and your own posts and comments can be deleted.

Depending on where you live, the law may also give you rights to see, correct, or object to how your data is used. The controls above cover most of them; email us for anything they do not.
