# RepSearch Health and Safety Disclaimer

_Last updated: October 5, 2026._

RepSearch is a workout-tracking and comparison app. It is not a medical service. This page is part of the [RepSearch Terms of Use](../terms/).

## Not medical advice

RepSearch does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and nothing in the app replaces a doctor, physiotherapist, or other qualified professional. Do not use a number, a standing, a program, or a comment in RepSearch to make a decision about your health.

## Before you train

Talk to a qualified professional before starting or changing a training routine, especially if you:

- have a heart, blood pressure, joint, or other medical condition;
- are recovering from an injury or surgery;
- are pregnant or recently gave birth;
- take medication that affects exertion; or
- have not trained in a long time.

## While you train

Strength training carries a risk of injury. You choose the exercises, the loads, and how hard you push, and you train at your own risk.

- Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, chest discomfort, or shortness of breath beyond normal effort, and get medical help.
- Use equipment you know how to use, and use safeties or a spotter on lifts where you could be pinned.
- A logged set, a previous-performance hint, a target, or a rest timer is a record or a convenience. It is not an instruction to attempt a lift.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## What the numbers mean

- **Estimated one-rep max** is calculated from a logged set. It is not a tested maximum, and effort, technique, and rep choice all affect it.
- **Standing, percentile, tier, figure, and color** describe where your logged training sits next to a comparison group or, early on, next to estimated starting levels that the app labels as estimates. They are not a judgement of your health or fitness and not a prediction of what you will lift.
- **Next-target lines** show what would move your standing. They are arithmetic on your own log, not a recommendation to lift that load.
- **Recorded results** on a program page describe what members who ran that version logged. They do not show that the program caused the result, and they are not a reason to expect the same.

## Programs and workouts

Programs and workouts in Discover are written and published by members. Their reasoning is the creator's own. RepSearch does not review them for safety, does not check that they suit your experience, health, or equipment, and does not recommend one over another. Adjust or stop any program when you need to.

## Bodyweight

Bodyweight you enter is used only to place your lifts in a bodyweight-relative class and to show your own trend. RepSearch does not give weight, diet, or nutrition advice.
