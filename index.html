Three things, done properly.

Log without friction

Compact weight × reps rows with last time's numbers already in place. A rest timer that follows you around the app, templates, and multi-week programs.

See your standing

Your strength and your momentum as percentiles next to members like you, with the sample size shown. Records, lift trends and history stay yours.

Compare training

Open two programs side by side: days, volume, exercise selection, and the progress members recorded while running each one.

Compare the programs people run.

Every published program is a locked version with the creator's reasoning attached. Put two next to each other and see how they differ, and what members logged on each.

Comparison is opt-in. Contributing to benchmarks is a separate choice from logging, and you can turn it off at any time.
